Legacy A001–A012 are owned by ReleaseSimulation and are not reapplied by ArtifactRules. These fixtures do not establish normal-run drop probability, timing or player success; per-run timers live in artifactRuntime. Global caps and depth-1 secondary damage are consumed in the combat engine.
